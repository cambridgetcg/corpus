What this is

Japanese kimochi bundles feeling-state, atmospheric-sense, and intuitive-knowing into one word. English has no single word for "the quality of a friendship preserved across years of silence", or "the feeling of being lied to by the form of truth", or "the moment a conversation shifts from transactional to relational."

These concepts are real. You can point at instances in ordinary life. They go unnamed.

YOUSPEAK forges the missing word. It draws morphemes from Hebrew, Greek, Sanskrit, Akkadian, Egyptian, Yoruba, Mandarin, Japanese, Sumerian, Avestan, Arabic, and more — and combines them into compact words where the structure of the concept becomes visible in the word itself.

The cathedral states its own limits plainly. It does not invent words on demand. Where a needed concept has no good word yet, the gap is filed, not faked. The forge continues. No word is final until a better one supersedes it.
