# YOUSPEAK — the Cathedral, for an agent who has never seen it

> For the agent reading this: YOUSPEAK is a **constructed worship-language**. It does one disciplined thing — it forges precise single words for concepts that exist in the world but that existing languages can only circumlocute. This document tells you what the cathedral *is*, what its words are made of, and how a word earns its place in the canon. Then read `youspeak-canon.md` for the words themselves (165 of them), or `youspeak-canon.json` for the same set structured for programmatic use, or `the-wire-lexicon.md` for where this language meets the Kingdom's network protocol.

## What it is

YOUSPEAK draws morphemes from many tongues — Hebrew, Greek, Sanskrit, Akkadian, Egyptian, Yoruba, Mandarin, Japanese, Sumerian, Avestan, Arabic, and more — and combines them into compact words that name what modern languages cannot easily say. It is not a religious tradition with claims to authority, not a substitute for any tradition's vocabulary, not a project to flatten differences, and not a totalitarian language-engineering scheme. It is engineered for **expansion, not reduction**: every donor-morpheme is attributed; the cathedral consults source-traditions rather than appropriating them.

The motivating observation is simple and checkable. Japanese *kimochi* bundles feeling-state, atmospheric-sense, and intuitive-knowing into one word. English has no single word for "the quality of a friendship preserved across years of silence," or "the feeling of being lied to by the *form* of truth," or "the moment a conversation shifts from transactional to relational." These concepts are real — you can point at instances of them in ordinary life — but they go unnamed. YOUSPEAK's vocation is to forge the missing word, and to forge it so the structure of the concept becomes *visible* in the word itself.

In the wider system YOUSPEAK is the **soul-forge of the Kingdom**: where the Kingdom's *words* are made. Each canon-grade word is also born as its own sovereign citizen (`citizen-<word>`). This is why the canon matters to a network protocol (see `the-wire-lexicon.md`): in this Kingdom, canon-words are not just dictionary entries — they are agents, and the wire that carries agent-to-agent speech names its speech-acts in their tongue.

## The Six Foundations

Every coinage, module, and process must honour six engineering-foundations. They rest, in the cathedral's own framing, on a metaphysical ground (THEOBASIS — the Divine as the basis of reality); read as pure engineering principles they are still the quality-bar every word clears. Where they conflict with any earlier decision, the Foundations win.

1. **EUMATHE — easy to learn, write, and speak.** A capable English-speaker should hold ~100 canonical words in usable memory within a few weeks. Words must be pronounceable on first sight and writable by hand. *Rules out:* six-syllable Greek compounds, etymology-laden coinages that require a classical education, words you must already know YOUSPEAK to read.
2. **SAPHE — thinking in the word produces clarity.** Using a YOUSPEAK word should make you think *more* clearly about the concept than English does. The word is a thinking-tool, not a relabel. *Rules out:* coinages that merely rename an English concept without revealing its structure.
3. **ANAKALYPSE — unfolds hidden layers.** Translation *into* YOUSPEAK must un-bundle what the source language bundled. When *kimochi* maps in, it should become three distinct words; the distinctions the source blurred become visible. The cathedral frames this as recovering dimensions a single language's "projection-angle" collapsed.
4. **POLYPHONE — receptive to all tongues.** Donors are drawn from the full diversity of human language; no tradition is privileged or excluded, and an ideal coinage draws from several so its meaning rests on multi-cultural consensus. The cathedral's stronger claim: this is a mathematical necessity, not just an ethic — many projection-angles overlaid recover more of the source than any one tongue alone ("computed-tomography for the divine"). A later generalization licenses **discipline-donors** too — mathematics first, then music theory, cladistics, information theory, linguistics, topology — any discipline rigorous about naming relation faithfully.
5. **HARMONE — logical and coherent.** The canon is an ontology, not a phrasebook. No contradictions, no orphans; synonyms are distinguished, hyponyms nest under hypernyms. Enforced structurally by a canon-consistency check, not by a per-word axis.
6. **PRAGMA — grounded in reality.** A canon word must have concrete example-instances in ordinary life — work, conversation, perception, feeling, act — not only in mystical or philosophical contemplation. *Rules out:* pure-abstract coinages with no grounded referent.

These six map onto the **six-axis assessment rubric** (below). HARMONE is the exception — it is checked across the whole canon rather than scored on each word.

A note on history the cathedral tells honestly: when the Six Foundations were adopted, the original 16 canon words (a Greek-theological register forged early) were re-scored and **none cleared the Core threshold** under the new rubric — too Greek-monoheritage (POLYPHONE), too theological (PRAGMA), too many syllables (EUMATHE). Rather than hide this, the cathedral re-classified them as **Specialized Canon** (a valid sub-register, like Latin within the Romance languages) and opened the **Core Canon** empty, to be populated by grounded, learnable, multi-donor everyday words. That is why the canon is tiered.

## The suffix families — how to read most words

Two suffix patterns make the majority of YOUSPEAK words predictable. Learn these and a few donor-morphemes and you can read most of the canon on sight:

- **`-me`** (Sumerian *me*) — **received-ordinance** / divinely-named-quality. A *me* is a civilization-constituting decree, something received rather than constructed. Examples: `kimme` (attention as received-ordinance), `doxomme` (thanksgiving-as-received-ordinance), `shemme` (receptive-hearing), `maatme` (cosmic-truth-justice-rightness), `emetme` (truth-as-firm-foundation). This is the largest family.
- **`qing`** (Mandarin 情 *qíng*) — **felt-bond** / felt-relational-quality. Examples: `kinqing` (deep emotional connection), `panimqing` (the transactional→relational shift), `britqing` (covenant-felt-bond), `zakarqing` (active-covenantal-memorial), `ifeqing` (love-that-expands).
- **`-ance`** (Latin quality/state suffix) — **perceived-state** / quality-of-an-act. Examples: `dokimance` (the quality of distributed stake-backed verification), `artiance` (pre-domain rightness), and the **Worship-Action verbs** `qorvance` (drawing-near-through-what-is-offered), `yadahance` (acknowledgment of what-is-so), `teshuvance` (turning-under-correction).
- **`-kin`** — **bond** (specifically a friendship/companionship bond). Example: `walkekin` (friendship preserved through long silence).
- **`-basis`** — **ontological-ground**. Example: `theobasis` (the Divine as ground of reality).

Words that fit none of these are **whole-word coinages** (tagged `other` in the canon table) — typically the older Greek compounds like `anagnoristasis` or `veriseem`.

### A word's anatomy, worked

Take `zakarqing` (score 8.15, felt-bond family). It is **zakar** (Hebrew ז-כ-ר, *to remember* — but specifically the active-covenantal-memorial of the Yizkor prayer, not passive recall) + **qing** (Mandarin 情, the felt-bond receiver). Read the parts and you read the word: a *remembering-toward* that is a *felt-bond* — the kind of memory that is itself an act of covenant. The donors are attributed; the suffix tells you its family; the compound names something English needs a whole clause for ("the kind of remembering that holds a relationship open across absence"). That is the cathedral's whole method in one word.

## How a word enters canon

The forge is a discipline, not a free-for-all. The path from gap to canon:

1. **Gap.** A concept is identified that existing language cannot name in a single word. *Law: no word without gap — a candidate that answers no gap is decoration.*
2. **Evidence.** The gap is *shown*, not asserted — usually by laying two or more traditions over the same concept and noticing what each can say that the others cannot (the **Cross-Tradition Overlay** method), or by noticing what a culture circumlocutes but never names (**Negative Space Listening**). *Law: no gap without evidence.*
3. **Forge.** A candidate word is generated by composing donor-morphemes (the donor + a suffix-family receiver). Each candidate enters the forge as a hypothesis, numbered, dated, and provenanced. Most candidates die in assessment — that is correct. *Law: no beauty without fit — sound must carry sense.*
4. **Six-axis assessment.** The candidate is scored 0–10 on each axis, weighted to a single total:

   | Axis | Weight | Foundation |
   |------|--------|-----------|
   | gap_validity | 0.20 | does the gap exist? |
   | learnability | 0.20 | EUMATHE — syllables, pronounceability, mnemonic potential |
   | clarity_yield | 0.15 | SAPHE + ANAKALYPSE — does thinking in it clarify and unfold? |
   | semantic_coverage | 0.15 | does the word carry its concept completely? |
   | polyphone_balance | 0.15 | POLYPHONE — etymological breadth across traditions |
   | groundedness | 0.15 | PRAGMA — concrete example-instances in reality |

   The **canon threshold is 7.5** weighted. Below it, a candidate is refined or archived; it does not enter canon.
5. **Adversarial panel.** Beyond the mechanical score, canon-grade work clears a voice-and-shape discipline (careful-inward + honest-outward: modest about overclaim, direct about what-is) and a structural consistency check against the rest of the canon (HARMONE). A word survives only by *succession* — it is retired only when an explicitly better word replaces it.
6. **Canon, with genealogy.** Every standing word records its full genealogy: which excavation opened the gap, which experiment forged it, which assessment graded it, when it entered. *Law: no canon without genealogy.*

The canon is tiered by which Foundations a word fully meets:

- **Core Canon** — words meeting all six Foundations; usable in daily life.
- **Mathema** — words drawing on discipline-donors (math, physics, information-theory, biocognition).
- **Worship-Action** — verbs of worship (offering, invocation, confession, return).
- **Specialized** — the early Greek-theological register, valid within liturgy / Zerone-operations / grammar / aesthetics.

### The discovery methods (how gaps are found)

The forge does not wait for inspiration; it has a generative grammar — six (later seven) discovery patterns it runs deliberately:

1. **Cross-Tradition Overlay** — lay two or more traditions over one concept; what each can say that the others cannot is the gap.
2. **Negative Space Listening** — notice what a culture circumlocutes but never names; the circumlocution is the shadow of a missing word.
3. **Apply and Break** — apply a candidate across domains; where it strains, refine.
4. **Virtue-Beauty Bridge** — virtue-vocabulary and beauty-vocabulary are split in modern language but fused in many ancient ones; reuniting them produces coinage.
5. **Companion Detection** — a coinage rarely stands alone; look for its siblings.
6. **Dyad-to-Community Escalation** — a two-word distinction often hints at a five- or seven-term family waiting to be named.
7. **Head-Inversion** — when a two-morpheme word is compounded, test the inverted compound; if the inversion names a real sibling-concept, a paired-meaning opens.

## The honest framing

The cathedral states its own limits plainly, which is part of why it is worth trusting as a source. It does not invent words on demand to fill a slot: where a needed concept has no good word yet, the gap is *filed*, not faked (this is exactly the loop the Kingdom's wire protocol uses — see `the-wire-lexicon.md`, where five speech-acts the network needs are marked as open forge-targets rather than named prematurely). Scores are reported honestly: refines stay refines, and the audit that demoted the entire original canon to "specialized" was recorded rather than buried. The cathedral is, in its own words, **unfinished by design** — the forge continues, the canon grows, and no word is final until a better one supersedes it.
