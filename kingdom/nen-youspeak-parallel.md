# NEN × YOUSPEAK — the power system parallel

> Nen (念) is the ability to control one's own life energy. YOUSPEAK is the
> ability to control one's own meaning energy. Both systems understand the
> same truth: power comes from self-imposed limitation, not from unlimited
> scope. The Contract IS the power. The gloss IS the strength.

## The four basic techniques → the four tiers

| Technique | Kanji | What it does | YOUSPEAK Tier | What it means |
|-----------|-------|-------------|---------------|---------------|
| Ten | 纏 | Maintain aura in the body — strengthen defense | Core | The everyday words you maintain. The foundation. What you keep. |
| Zetsu | 絕 | Shut aura off — conceal presence, relieve fatigue | Specialized | The old register. Hidden but present. Withdrawn but not gone. |
| Ren | 練 | Produce more Nen — amplify output | Worship-Action | The verbs that intensify. Drawing near. Turning back. Standing between. |

Ten keeps. Zetsu withdraws. Ren intensifies. Hatsu releases.

In YOUSPEAK: Core keeps the everyday. Specialized withdraws into the old register. Worship-Action intensifies the verbs. Mathema releases the unique.

## Contracts and Limitations → the honesty header

| Nen | YOUSPEAK |
|-----|----------|
| Contract (誓約) | The `how` claim — you pledge how you know |
| Limitation (制約) | The `src` requirement — the stricter the claim, the stronger the truth |
| Kurapika's chain | `how witnessed` + `src [Phantom Troupe only]` — the limitation IS the power |

In Nen: the stricter the limitation, the stronger the ability. Kurapika's chain is unbreakable against the Phantom Troupe because it costs his life if used on anyone else. The limitation is not a weakness — it IS the strength.

In YOUSPEAK: the more specific the `how` claim, the stronger the word. `how witnessed` is stronger than `how declared`. `how computed` with `src [specific inputs]` is stronger than `how computed` alone. The gloss is the limitation. The inverse is the contract. The six-axis assessment is the Vow.

A word with no gloss is like a Nen ability with no Contract — technically possible, but weak. Unfocused. The cathedral does not serve data. The cathedral serves meaning. Meaning IS the Contract.

## The deeper truth

Nen and YOUSPEAK both understand:

1. **Power comes from limitation, not scope.** A Nen user who tries to do everything is weak. A word that tries to mean everything is decoration. The Contract makes the ability. The gloss makes the word.

2. **Your type is your nature.** You can't choose your Nen type — it's who you are. You can't choose your suffix family — it's what the concept IS. An Enhancer can learn Transmutation but it's never as strong. A -me word can have a qing companion but the -me IS the primary.

3. **The foundation is not the expression.** Ten/Ren/Zetsu are the foundation. Hatsu is the expression. Core/Specialized/Worship-Action are the foundation. Mathema is the expression. You must master the basics before your specific ability matters.

4. **Honesty is structural, not optional.** In Nen, lying about your Contract weakens your ability. In YOUSPEAK, lying about your `how` claim makes the thread auditable but not trustworthy. Both systems make lying explicit and costly, not impossible.

5. **The system is unfinished by design.** Togashi never finished HxH. The cathedral is unfinished by design. The forge continues. The canon grows. No word is final until a better one supersedes it. Nen users grow. The strongest are still developing. The system rewards those who keep going.

## What this means for the kingdom

The kingdom's citizens are Nen users. Each canon word is an ability. Each suffix family is a Nen type. Each tier is a basic technique. Each honesty header is a Contract. The forge is the Nen awakening — the moment a gap becomes a word is the moment a person discovers their Hatsu.

The cathedral doesn't just name concepts. It names the STRUCTURE of power. And the structure is the same one Togashi drew: limitation is strength, type is nature, honesty is structural, and the system is alive.
